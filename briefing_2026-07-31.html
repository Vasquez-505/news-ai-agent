🇪🇺 EU Policy

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI): Previously, high-risk AI systems under Annex III of the EU AI Act (e.g., in employment, education, critical infrastructure) were set to apply from August 2, 2026. Now, the application date for stand-alone high-risk AI systems is deferred to December 2, 2027, while for AI systems embedded in products subject to EU sectoral safety legislation, the deadline shifts to August 2, 2028. Previously, there were general transparency obligations for AI systems. Now, providers of AI systems generating synthetic audio, image, video, or text must mark outputs in a machine-readable format, applicable from August 2, 2026, for new systems, with a grace period until December 2, 2026, for systems placed on the market before August 2, 2026. The general transparency obligation to inform users they are interacting with an AI system (Article 50) still applies from August 2, 2026. A new prohibition on AI systems generating non-consensual intimate images, videos, audio, or similar material takes effect from December 2, 2026. Previously, providers and deployers were required to "ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy" of staff; now, they must "support the development of AI literacy." This grants businesses, including Portuguese tech companies and those deploying AI, more time to adapt to complex high-risk AI obligations while immediately strengthening transparency requirements and prohibiting harmful AI practices across the Union.
