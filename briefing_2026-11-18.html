🌍 World News

Palestine/Europe: Ireland's Taoiseach Simon Harris, Norway's PM Jonas Gahr Støre, and Spain's PM Pedro Sánchez announced on May 22 that their nations will formally recognize a Palestinian state on May 28. This coordinated move marks the first time three major EU members have acted in unison to recognize Palestinian statehood outside the context of a negotiated settlement with Israel. Israel responded by immediately recalling its ambassadors from Oslo, Madrid, and Dublin. This shift challenges the long-standing Western diplomatic consensus that recognition must only follow a bilateral agreement. Reuters

United Kingdom: Prime Minister Rishi Sunak announced on May 22 that a general election will be held on July 4, 2024. The decision follows a period of economic instability and intense pressure from within the Conservative Party to set a firm date. The UK's major political parties began deploying candidates across 650 constituencies immediately following the announcement. This election will determine the UK's regulatory and trade trajectory with the European Union and the United States for the next five years. BBC

Nvidia/AI Economy: Nvidia reported first-quarter revenue of $26.0 billion on May 22, representing a 262% increase compared to the previous year. CEO Jensen Huang also confirmed a 10-for-1 stock split taking effect on June 7 to make shares more accessible to retail investors. The company's share price surged past $1,000 in after-hours trading following the release. As the primary provider of AI chips, Nvidia's growth serves as the definitive benchmark for global investment in artificial intelligence infrastructure. Financial Times

Iran: The Guardian Council of Iran officially scheduled the presidential election for June 28, 2024, to replace the late President Ebrahim Raisi. The council is currently reviewing applications from candidates to ensure they meet strict ideological requirements. Security forces increased patrols in Tehran following small-scale protests against the candidate selection process. The outcome will signal whether Iran maintains its current hardline foreign policy or shifts its approach toward the West and regional rivals. Al Jazeera

🇺🇸 US Policy

Section 301 Tariff Modifications: The USTR finalized tariff increases on specific Chinese imports, raising duties on electric vehicles (EVs) to 100%, lithium-ion EV batteries to 25%, and certain semiconductors to 50%. Previously: EV tariffs were 25%; now they are 100% effective for 2024. This increases the cost of Chinese EV imports to protect US domestic automotive production and supply chains. Reuters

OFAC Russia-Related Procurement Sanctions: The Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) designated 12 entities located in the UAE and Turkey for facilitating the transfer of dual-use electronics to Russia. Previously: These entities were not sanctioned; now they are added to the Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list, blocking all US assets and prohibiting transactions effective immediately. This tightens the restriction of critical components for Russian defense systems, impacting global logistics and trade intermediaries. Treasury.gov

FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) Rule: The Treasury Department implemented the final regulatory framework under the Corporate Transparency Act requiring most small businesses and LLCs to report beneficial ownership information to FinCEN. Previously: No federal requirement existed for most small LLCs to disclose ownership to the government; now it is mandatory for most entities registered in the US effective as of the current reporting cycle. This increases transparency in corporate ownership but raises administrative compliance costs for small-cap and private holdings. Federal Register

Why it matters: The US is aggressively utilizing targeted tariffs and sanctions to decouple critical technology supply chains from China and Russia.
