⚡ AI & Productivity

Meta: Meta released Muse Glimmer-30B, a 30-billion-parameter open-weight causal language model with a dedicated perception encoder, distilled from Muse Spark, designed for autonomous agentic tasks. This model is purpose-built to run locally on consumer hardware like Macs or PCs with a single graphics card, integrating multi-step reasoning, tool use, and multimodal understanding, which differs from larger, cloud-dependent models. Developers can now build and deploy sophisticated AI agents that perform complex, multi-step tasks directly on personal computers, enabling privacy-preserving workflows and reducing reliance on costly cloud-based inference.

xAI: xAI launched Grok Bot in beta, an AI agent product that functions as an always-on, unsupervised digital workforce, capable of signing into a user's existing tools and completing multi-step jobs. This offering extends xAI's product line beyond conversational AI models into direct "digital labor," providing autonomous execution of workflows instead of just generating responses. Knowledge workers can delegate complex, multi-application tasks that previously required manual oversight, such as automatically generating a project report by synthesizing data across various platforms.

NVIDIA: NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (30B A3B) BF16, an open-weight, 30-billion-parameter language model featuring a hybrid Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture with 3 billion active parameters. This model supports a context length of up to 1 million tokens (or 256K for single H100 deployment) and is provided with full-precision BF16 reference weights, making it an ideal starting point for customization, post-training, and research. Developers can leverage this efficient, open-source model to fine-tune highly specialized AI agents for specific enterprise domains or create quantized versions for edge devices, enabling custom, performant AI solutions at reduced inference costs.

Why it matters: The increasing focus on open-weight, locally deployable, and autonomous agentic AI models is empowering developers and knowledge workers to build specialized, privacy-preserving, and cost-efficient AI solutions that directly integrate into existing workflows without relying solely on large, proprietary cloud APIs.

🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

Despacho Normativo n.º 9/2026 – Rede de Refúgios Climáticos «Stay Cool»: Este despacho abre um aviso específico de concurso, ao abrigo do Programa Crescer com o Turismo, para a criação de uma rede nacional de espaços seguros e termicamente confortáveis, que funcionarão como refúgios climáticos em períodos de calor extremo. Anteriormente, não existia um programa específico com este foco e apoio para a criação de "refúgios climáticos". Agora, as autarquias podem aceder a apoio técnico e financeiro para identificar, qualificar, sinalizar e gerir estes espaços. O impacto prático é o aumento da segurança e conforto de residentes e turistas durante ondas de calor, e o reforço da resiliência dos destinos turísticos.

Decreto-Lei n.º 164/2026 – Simplificação de Procedimentos nas Secretarias Judiciais e do Ministério Público: Este decreto-lei visa simplificar os procedimentos internos das secretarias judiciais e dos serviços do Ministério Público, em particular no que respeita às custas processuais. Anteriormente, as partes frequentemente necessitavam de requerer ou aguardar um despacho judicial para obter as guias de pagamento de custas. Agora, a regra geral é que a secretaria deve fornecer as guias de pagamento proativamente, sem necessidade de requerimento ou despacho judicial prévio, sempre que a obrigação de pagamento seja determinada por notificação da secretaria ou decorra da lei com prazo a contar da notificação. A sua aplicação dispensa as partes do esforço adicional de obtenção do documento de autoliquidação. O impacto prático para cidadãos e empresas é uma redução da burocracia e uma maior celeridade e transparência nos processos de pagamento de custas judiciais.

Portaria n.º 328/2026/1 – Regulamento da Pesca na Ria Formosa: Esta portaria aprova as novas normas reguladoras da pesca comercial e estabelece regras para o exercício da pesca lúdica nas águas interiores não marítimas da Ria Formosa. A portaria anterior, n.º 560/90, de 19 de julho, que aprovava o Regulamento da Pesca da Ria Formosa, é revogada. O impacto prático é a alteração do enquadramento legal para pescadores comerciais e lúdicos nesta região específica, o que pode implicar novas restrições ou permissões e exigir uma adaptação às novas regras de exploração do recurso.

Lei n.º 45/2026 – Carreira Especial de Médico Dentista no Serviço Nacional de Saúde: Esta lei estabelece e regulamenta a carreira especial de médico dentista no Serviço Nacional de Saúde (SNS). Anteriormente, não existia uma carreira específica e formalmente estruturada para médicos dentistas no SNS. Agora, é criada uma carreira própria para estes profissionais. O impacto prático é a melhoria da capacidade do SNS para atrair e reter médicos dentistas, visando o reforço e a melhoria da oferta de cuidados de saúde oral para os cidadãos.

🇪🇺 EU Policy

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU Artificial Intelligence Act) – Transparency Obligations: Providers of AI systems interacting directly with individuals must now disclose they are engaging with AI, unless already obvious. Providers of AI systems generating or manipulating synthetic audio, image, video, or text must embed machine-readable markings and provide a detection mechanism. Deployers of emotion recognition or biometric categorization systems, as well as those distributing deepfakes or publishing AI-generated text for public interest, must also make specific disclosures. Previously, there was no specific EU-wide legal framework for transparency obligations of AI systems, leading to varied practices. Now, these specific disclosure and marking rules are in application, with non-compliance potentially triggering fines up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover. The obligations apply from August 2, 2026, though a grace period until December 2, 2026, is granted for watermarking and detection obligations of generative AI systems placed on the market before August 2, 2026. This is highly relevant for Portuguese tech companies and businesses using AI for customer interaction or content generation, requiring immediate compliance review.

Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation - PPWR): New rules concerning packaging will apply across the EU, introducing a restriction on per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) in food-contact packaging, meaning such packaging with PFAS above strict limits can no longer be placed on the EU market. Furthermore, companies must now hold documentation confirming packaging compliance, including the EU Declaration of Conformity and Technical Documentation, and ensure adherence to PFAS and heavy metal limits. For importers from outside the EU, it is essential to appoint an authorized representative for Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) in each Member State to which they export. Previously, packaging rules were governed by Directive 94/62/EC, which allowed for diverse national implementations and did not include specific EU-wide PFAS restrictions in food-contact packaging. These new requirements apply from August 12, 2026, directly impacting all businesses placing packaging on the EU market, particularly those in the food sector. Portuguese businesses must urgently review their packaging supply chains and compliance documentation.

Directive (EU) 2024/825 (Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition Directive): This Directive, formally adopted, will apply in its entirety from September 27, 2026, requiring Member States to transpose it into national law. It amends the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive (2005/29/EC) by introducing stricter requirements for environmental claims: businesses will no longer be permitted to use sustainability labels that are not based on an established certification scheme or issued by a public authority. Previously, environmental claims were largely subject to general unfair commercial practices rules, allowing for varied interpretations and instances of unsubstantiated "greenwashing." This change will significantly affect consumer-facing businesses across all sectors in Portugal, mandating a review of their marketing materials, product labeling, and sustainability communications to ensure claims are substantiated.

Regulation (EU) 2023/1543 (e-Evidence Regulation): The core provisions of this regulation will become applicable on August 18, 2026. It will allow judicial authorities in one Member State to directly request electronic evidence (such as subscriber, traffic, or content data) from service providers located in another Member State, using a European Production Order (EPOC). Previously, cross-border requests for electronic evidence required lengthy mutual legal assistance procedures between Member States. This streamlined process will expedite law enforcement investigations across the EU, affecting Portuguese service providers by mandating a direct response to EPOCs from other Member States.

Why it matters: This week sees the direct application of significant new digital and environmental regulations, including key transparency obligations under the AI Act and harmonized packaging rules, alongside the formal approval of new consumer protection standards and the imminent application of streamlined cross-border evidence requests, fundamentally altering compliance landscapes for businesses across the EU.

FTC Policy Statement Regarding Disparate-Impact and "Unfair Discrimination" Claims: The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) announced on August 7, 2026, a policy statement clarifying that it will no longer pursue legal claims based on disparate-impact or "unfair discrimination" theories under Section 5 of the FTC Act.
