Member States, including Portugal, have until September 5, 2028, to transpose these changes into national legislation, with full application by March 5, 2029. Now, the EU directive revising the lists of pollutants in surface and groundwaters entered into force on May 11, 2026. It requires Member States to monitor for an updated and expanded list of substances, including certain PFAS "forever chemicals," specific pesticides and pharmaceuticals, microplastics, and indicators of antimicrobial resistance. The directive also introduces "effect-based monitoring" to assess the combined risk to water quality, rather than solely individual pollutants, and streamlines some reporting obligations for Member States.
