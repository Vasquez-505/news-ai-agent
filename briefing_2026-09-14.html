Previously: No centralized EU risk-classification or mandatory pre-market registration for AI software. Now: High-risk systems must undergo rigorous technical documentation audits and human-oversight verification before deployment. Deadline: Immediate enforcement for high-risk categories as of September 2026. This increases the operational cost and legal liability for Portuguese AI tech startups and financial services providers. [EUR-Lex]

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD): Large undertakings (defined as those with >250 employees, assets >€25m, or turnover >€50m) must submit their first audited sustainability reports according to European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) for the 2025 fiscal year. Previously: Non-financial reporting was limited to public-interest entities under the NFRD with flexible, non-standardized metrics. Now: Mandatory "limited assurance" (external audit) is required for all sustainability data, covering carbon footprints and social impact. Deadline: September 30, 2026. This imposes a new, significant administrative cost on Portuguese mid-cap companies. [EUR-Lex]

Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EPBD): Owners of non-residential buildings over 250m² must implement energy efficiency upgrades to align with new Minimum Energy Performance Standards (MEPS). Previously: Energy upgrades for commercial properties were largely voluntary or driven by national tax incentives. Now: Mandatory reduction of average primary energy use by 16% by 2030, with a requirement to submit the first baseline energy performance audit by September 2026.
