USA/China Semiconductors: President Julian Thorne signed Executive Order 14150 on September 30, 2026, banning all exports of 2-nanometer semiconductor chips to Chinese firms. The order specifically targets fabrication plants operated by SMIC and Huawei in Shanghai and Shenzhen. Beijing retaliated by restricting gallium and germanium exports by 40% via the Ministry of Commerce. This escalation disrupts global consumer electronics supply chains and accelerates China's push for total chip autonomy. [Reuters]

BRICS+ Currency: Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and Chinese President Xi Jinping launched the "R-Unit" digital reserve currency for inter-governmental trade on September 30. The pilot program includes 12 member states and is backed by an initial liquidity pool of $450 billion in gold and diversified assets. Three G7 finance ministers issued a joint statement warning against the fragmentation of the international monetary system. The move reduces global reliance on the US Dollar as the primary reserve currency for commodity trades. [Financial Times]

Geneva AI Accord: Representatives from 84 nations signed the Geneva AI Accord on September 30, 2026, implementing a global moratorium on the development of autonomous lethal weapon systems. The treaty establishes an International AI Inspection Agency in Switzerland to perform mandatory audits on models exceeding $10 billion in training costs. OpenAI and Google DeepMind formally signed the accord's transparency protocols. This creates the first legally binding international framework to prevent an automated arms race. [AP]

Vietnam/Thailand Flooding: Typhoon Mawar-II made landfall in Central Vietnam and Eastern Thailand on September 30, displacing 1.2 million people. The Vietnamese Ministry of Agriculture confirmed the destruction of 450,000 hectares of rice crops in the Mekong Delta. The UN launched an immediate $200 million emergency appeal for food and medical supplies. The loss of crop yields will likely trigger a spike in global rice prices and destabilize Southeast Asian food security. [Al Jazeera]
