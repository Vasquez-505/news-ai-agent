🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

Pacote Fiscal para a Habitação: O Presidente da República promulgou em 12 de maio de 2026 um decreto-lei que aprova um conjunto de medidas de desagravamento fiscal para fomentar a oferta de habitação. Anteriormente, a taxa de IVA na construção de imóveis para habitação permanente era de 23%; agora, é reduzida para 6% para imóveis destinados à venda ou arrendamento a preços moderados (até €2300/mês para arrendamento e €660.982 para venda). A taxa autónoma de IRS sobre rendimentos prediais para proprietários que pratiquem rendas moderadas desce de 25% para 10%. É excluída a tributação de mais-valias sobre a venda de imóveis habitacionais quando o valor é reinvestido em novos imóveis para arrendamento. O limite da dedução de rendas no IRS, que era de €700, aumenta para €900 em 2026 e para €1.000 a partir de 2027. Para compradores estrangeiros não residentes, a taxa de IMT para aquisição de habitação passa a ser fixa em 7,5%, sem isenções ou reduções. Estas medidas visam incentivar o aumento da oferta de habitação a preços mais acessíveis e aliviar a carga fiscal para proprietários e construtores envolvidos no arrendamento moderado.

Revisão das Taxas Unitárias do Imposto sobre Produtos Petrolíferos e Energéticos (ISP): A Portaria n.º 222-B/2026/1, publicada em 15 de maio de 2026, procede à revisão e fixação das taxas unitárias do imposto sobre os produtos petrolíferos e energéticos. Esta medida implica uma atualização dos valores dos impostos aplicados aos combustíveis, o que terá um impacto direto nos custos de abastecimento para consumidores e empresas.

Autenticação de Dois Fatores (2FA) na Segurança Social Direta: A partir de 15 de maio de 2026, tornou-se obrigatória a utilização de dupla autenticação (2FA) para aceder ao portal da Segurança Social Direta. Anteriormente, o acesso dependia apenas de um NISS e palavra-passe; agora, exige-se uma camada adicional de segurança. Esta alteração reforça a proteção dos dados dos cidadãos e das empresas contra acessos indevidos, embora possa requerer um passo adicional no processo de login.

Why it matters: As medidas fiscais para a habitação representam o mais significativo conjunto de alterações na última semana, procurando mitigar a crise habitacional através de incentivos à construção e ao arrendamento acessível, impactando diretamente tanto cidadãos como empresas.

Directive revising lists of pollutants in surface and groundwaters: This Directive, which entered into force on May 11, 2026, requires Member States to adapt their national legislation to include new substances in their lists of water pollutants, subjecting them to stricter monitoring and control. Previously, existing directives (Water Framework Directive, Environmental Quality Standards Directive, Groundwater Directive) regulated certain pollutants. Now, the updated lists explicitly add substances like specific PFAS 'forever chemicals' (e.g., TFA), pesticides, pharmaceuticals, microplastics, indicators of antimicrobial resistance, and sensitive groundwater ecosystems. Member States must use River Basin Management Plans to protect and restore water bodies to good status. Businesses in sectors such as chemicals, pharmaceuticals, agriculture, and waste management across the EU, including Portugal, will face enhanced environmental performance and reporting requirements.

Amendments to the EU AI Act (Provisional Agreement): A provisional agreement was reached on May 7, 2026, on targeted amendments to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act). Previously, high-risk AI systems (listed in Annex III, covering areas like biometrics, employment, and critical infrastructure) had a compliance deadline of August 2, 2026. Now, this deadline is deferred to December 2, 2027. Rules for AI systems integrated into products (e.g., lifts, toys) will apply from August 2, 2028. Additionally, the amendments introduce new prohibitions against AI systems that generate non-consensual sexually explicit content ("nudification apps") or child sexual abuse material. Formal adoption and publication in the Official Journal are expected in the coming weeks. This provides businesses, especially Portuguese tech companies and AI developers, more time to adapt to stringent AI regulations and introduces clear boundaries for AI use.

EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) Application Dates Confirmed: On May 4, 2026, the European Commission published a simplification package for the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) and formally confirmed that there will be no further postponement of its application dates. Previously, there was uncertainty among businesses due to stakeholder criticism and earlier postponements. Now, large and medium-sized companies must comply with the EUDR from December 30, 2026. Micro and small operators (outside the timber sector) retain their deferred application date of June 30, 2027. This Commission decision removes ambiguity, requiring all affected operators and traders in the EU, including those in Portugal dealing with commodities like cattle, cocoa, coffee, palm oil, rubber, soy, and wood, to finalize their supply chain mapping and due diligence systems by the confirmed deadlines.

Why it matters: The most significant EU-level change this week is the entry into force of new water protection rules and the confirmed, albeit deferred, application of major AI and deforestation compliance obligations, solidifying the regulatory landscape for environmental protection and digital innovation across the Union.

It removes perceived operational constraints and reverses the elevation of conservation as a discrete "use" of public lands. The final rule becomes effective on June 11, 2026. Effective July 10, 2026, the rule grants USCIS broader authority to reject or deny immigration benefit requests if signature problems are identified, even after an application has been accepted for processing, a change from previous assumptions that acceptance implied no further signature issues. This action increases the onus on applicants and legal representatives to ensure strict compliance with signature requirements, raising the risk of rejections or denials and potentially increasing processing complexities.
