🇪🇺 EU Policy

AI Act Implementing Regulation on GPAI Transparency: Requires providers of general-purpose AI models with training compute exceeding 10^25 FLOPs to submit detailed technical summaries of training data to the EU AI Office. Previously: Voluntary disclosure of training data. Now: Mandatory disclosure for models exceeding the 10^25 FLOPs threshold. Compliance deadline: October 1, 2026. Affected: Large-scale AI developers. This increases transparency for investors monitoring systemic AI risks. [EUR-Lex]

Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EPBD) - MEPS: Requires all non-residential buildings over 250 square meters to achieve at least Energy Class E by 2030. Previously: No binding EU-wide minimum energy performance standards for commercial assets. Now: Mandatory minimum Class E requirement for buildings >250sqm. Transposition deadline: December 31, 2026. Affected: Commercial real estate owners and managers. This is particularly relevant for Portuguese investors holding older commercial portfolios in Lisbon and Porto. [Official Journal of the EU]

Financial Data Access (FIDA) Framework: Mandates financial institutions to provide real-time API access to customer financial data—including insurance and investment portfolios—to authorized third-party providers. Previously: Data sharing was limited primarily to payment accounts under PSD2. Now: Mandatory data sharing across all financial asset classes. Application date: January 1, 2027. Affected: Banks, insurance companies, and asset managers. This enables the creation of more sophisticated, integrated wealth management tools for EU citizens. [European Commission]

Why it matters: The EU is transitioning from broad legislative frameworks to concrete technical enforcement and mandatory thresholds, directly impacting the valuation of real estate and tech assets.

🇺🇸 US Policy

SEC Final Rule on AI Operational Transparency: The SEC enacted a final rule requiring all publicly traded companies with market caps over $1 billion to disclose the specific AI models utilized in core financial reporting and the percentage of quarterly revenue attributed to AI-generated services. Previously: Disclosure of AI usage was voluntary and fragmented across various 10-K sections. Now: Mandatory, standardized quarterly reporting is required starting January 1, 2027. This provides long-term investors with concrete data to distinguish genuine AI integration from "AI-washing" in corporate earnings. Federal Register.

Executive Order 14210 on Critical Mineral Security: The President signed EO 14210, directing the Department of Defense and Department of Commerce to allocate $5.2 billion in direct grants for the domestic processing of lithium and cobalt. Previously: Domestic scaling relied primarily on private capital and limited tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act. Now: Direct grants are available for facilities that maintain a minimum 70% US-labor workforce, effective immediately. This action reduces geopolitical supply chain risk for US battery manufacturers and electric vehicle producers. Whitehouse.gov.

Treasury OFAC Quantum Computing Export Restrictions: The Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) issued a final designation prohibiting the export of quantum processors exceeding 100 qubits to four specific state-linked entities in East Asia. Previously: These exports were permitted under a general research license. Now: All commercial sales of these specific processors to designated entities are prohibited without a special license from the Commerce Department, effective September 20, 2026. This restricts the immediate addressable market for high-end quantum computing hardware providers. Reuters US Politics.

Why it matters: The US government is aggressively transitioning from broad industrial incentives to targeted, mandatory transparency and security mandates in the AI and critical mineral sectors.
