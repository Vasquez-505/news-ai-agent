AI Act (EU 2024/1689) - High-Risk AI Obligations: Requires providers of high-risk AI systems (e.g., AI used in recruitment, credit scoring, or critical infrastructure) to implement mandatory risk management systems, high-quality training datasets, and human oversight mechanisms. Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) - Definitive Regime: Requires EU importers of carbon-intensive goods (steel, cement, aluminum, fertilizers, electricity, and hydrogen) to purchase and surrender CBAM certificates to cover the embedded carbon emissions of their imports. Previously: The transition phase (ending December 31, 2025) required only the reporting of emissions without financial payment. Now: Importers face full financial liability for emissions as of January 1, 2026, with quarterly reporting deadlines. [European Commission] Impact: Increases operational costs for Portuguese industrial importers and construction firms sourcing materials from non-EU countries.

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) - Large Undertakings Phase: Requires companies meeting at least two of three criteria (>250 employees, >€50m turnover, or >€25m total assets) to publish audited sustainability reports according to European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS). Previously: Large companies reported under the NFRD, which had fewer standardized metrics and no mandatory audit. [EUR-Lex] Impact: Significantly increases compliance and auditing costs for large Portuguese enterprises and their supply chain partners.

Why it matters: The EU has shifted from the legislative phase to the enforcement phase of its "Twin Transition," moving the burden from policy debate to mandatory corporate financial liability and strict technical compliance.
