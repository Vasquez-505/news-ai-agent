🌍 World News

BRICS+: Leaders of the expanded BRICS+ bloc finalized the "R-Coin" digital reserve currency during a summit in Kazan, Russia, on November 9. The agreement establishes a settlement asset backed by a basket of gold and rare earth metals, with a mandatory 40% gold weighting for all participating member states. The International Monetary Fund announced an immediate review of Special Drawing Rights (SDR) following projections that 15% of global reserves will shift to the R-Coin by 2030. This development challenges the US dollar's hegemony in international trade settlements and sovereign reserves. Financial Times

EU-India Trade: European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi signed the Comprehensive Strategic Trade Agreement in New Delhi on November 9. The treaty eliminates tariffs on 85% of industrial goods and allocates €40 billion toward the creation of a joint semiconductor manufacturing corridor. Indian equity markets rose 2.4% as investors reacted to the guaranteed EU capital inflows for the Bengaluru tech hub. This pact accelerates the diversification of critical electronics supply chains away from China. Reuters

UN AI Treaty: Sixty-two nations, including the United States and China, ratified the Geneva Convention on Autonomous Weaponry on November 9. The treaty prohibits the deployment of lethal autonomous weapons systems (LAWS) that operate without a documented "meaningful human command" trigger. The UN Security Council established a permanent monitoring body in Geneva to conduct quarterly audits of national AI military inventories. This agreement prevents an unconstrained AI arms race and reduces the risk of accidental systemic conflict. AP

ITER Fusion Project: The ITER project in France announced on November 9 that it achieved a sustained plasma fusion reaction for 15 minutes at 150 million degrees Celsius. This mark surpasses the 10-minute stability threshold previously identified by the project's engineers as the minimum requirement for commercial energy viability. Global energy futures for natural gas declined by 3% as analysts moved the projected date for commercial fusion power forward to 2035. Successful sustained fusion represents the eventual obsolescence of fossil fuels for baseload electricity. BBC

Why it matters: The simultaneous shift toward a non-dollar reserve currency and the acceleration of fusion energy signal a fundamental restructuring of the global economic and geopolitical power balance.

🇪🇺 EU Policy

AI Act (High-Risk Systems Compliance): Providers of AI systems classified as "high-risk" must now complete mandatory conformity assessments and register their systems in the EU database to maintain market access. Previously: No unified EU framework existed for AI risk classification or mandatory pre-market certification. Now: High-risk providers must implement a full quality management system and technical documentation by November 10, 2026, or face fines up to €35 million or 7% of global turnover. This significantly increases the compliance cost for Portuguese AI startups and tech investors scaling high-risk healthcare or biometric tools. [EUR-Lex]

Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD - Phase 1): Companies with >5,000 employees and a net worldwide turnover of >€1,500 million must now formally integrate environmental and human rights due diligence into their corporate policies. Previously: Sustainability reporting was largely voluntary or limited to financial disclosure (CSRD). Now: These firms must identify and mitigate adverse impacts across their entire global value chain, with a compliance deadline for the first reporting cycle ending in late 2026. Large Portuguese exporters and industrial groups must now map every tier of their supply chain or risk civil liability. [European Commission]

MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets) Technical Standards for Stablecoins: New Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS) now mandate that Asset-Referenced Token (ART) issuers maintain a liquidity reserve of at least 110% of the value of the assets they reference. Previously: Reserve requirements were based on general prudential guidelines with lower, non-standardized thresholds. Now: A strict 110% liquidity minimum is required as of November 1, 2026, to prevent "bank runs" on stablecoins. This impacts Portuguese crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) by increasing capital requirements for token issuance. [EUR-Lex]

Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EPBD - Public Sector Mandate): Member States must now ensure that public building owners renovate at least 3% of the total floor area of their building stock annually to achieve zero-emission standards. Previously: Public buildings had generic energy reduction targets without a fixed annual floor-area quota. Now: A mandatory 3% minimum annual renovation rate is in force as of November 2026. This creates a guaranteed, long-term pipeline of government contracts for Portuguese construction and green-tech firms specializing in building retrofitting. [Official Journal of the EU]

Why it matters: The EU has shifted from the "legislative phase" to the "enforcement phase," replacing voluntary sustainability and tech guidelines with strict, quantified mandates and heavy turnover-based fines.

Previously: 48 firms were restricted from receiving US-made lithography equipment. Now: 60 firms are restricted, effective November 9, 2026. This likely increases revenue volatility for US chip equipment manufacturers relying on those specific foreign markets. Federal Register.

Federal Reserve Basel III Final Rule: The Federal Reserve Board finalized the "Capital Adequacy Framework" updating risk-weighting for operational risk. Previously: The proposed rule required a 16% increase in Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital for G-SIBs. Now: The final rule mandates a 9% increase, effective January 1, 2027. This reduces the projected capital drag on the largest US banks, potentially supporting higher share buyback levels. Federal Reserve Board.

SEC Climate Disclosure Final Rule: The SEC adopted a final rule requiring mandatory disclosure of Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions for large accelerated filers. Previously: Such disclosures were voluntary or based on inconsistent framework standards. Now: Mandatory filing in annual reports is required for companies with revenues exceeding $1 billion, with the first reports due for the 2027 fiscal year. This increases the regulatory compliance burden and legal liability for heavy-industrial equities. SEC.gov.

EO 141XX - National Quantum Computing Initiative: The President signed an Executive Order mandating the Department of Energy and NIST to establish a National Quantum Backbone. Previously: Quantum networking research was fragmented across individual agency grants. Now: A centralized $5.2 billion federal procurement mandate for inter-state quantum-secure communication links is established, effective immediately. This creates a guaranteed federal revenue stream for specialized quantum hardware and encryption vendors. Whitehouse.gov.

Why it matters: The simultaneous tightening of tech exports and the easing of bank capital requirements signal a strategic pivot toward domestic financial stability and "strategic autonomy" in critical technology.
