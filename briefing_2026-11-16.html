🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

Decreto-Lei n.º 112/2026 (Energy Efficiency Credits): Increases the personal income tax (IRS) credit for energy efficiency renovations in rented properties. Previously: 15% of expenses. Now: 25% of expenses, capped at €2,500 per property. Enters into force on December 1, 2026. This incentivizes landlords to modernize older building stock to meet updated climate standards. Diário da República.

Lei n.º 44/2026 (Startup Scale-up Incentive): Reduces the autonomous tax rate on distributed profits for certified "Scale-up" companies. Previously: 28% flat rate. Now: 15% for dividends distributed within the first 60 months of certification. In force as of November 12, 2026. This increases the net yield for venture capital investors in high-growth Portuguese tech firms. Jornal de Negócios.

Portaria n.º 310/2026 (2027 Minimum Wage): Establishes the National Minimum Wage (RMMG) for the upcoming calendar year. Previously: €870/month. Now: €920/month (a 5.7% increase) effective January 1, 2027. This will increase operational overhead for labor-intensive businesses across all sectors. Portugal.gov.pt.

Despacho n.º 891/2026 (VAT Exemption Threshold): Raises the annual turnover threshold for VAT exemption for self-employed workers under Article 53. Previously: €14,500. Now: €16,000. In force as of November 10, 2026. This reduces the administrative and accounting burden for approximately 120,000 low-income freelancers. Diário da República.

Why it matters: The reduction of the autonomous tax rate for Scale-ups significantly improves the after-tax ROI for investors in the Portuguese technology sector.
