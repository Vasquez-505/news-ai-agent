CSRD (Reporting Standard for Large Undertakings): Mandates that all large companies meeting at least two of three criteria—over 250 employees, €50m annual turnover, or €25m total assets—publish audited sustainability reports according to ESRS standards. Previously: The NFRD only required reporting for public-interest entities with >500 employees. Now: Mandatory reporting is expanded to all large undertakings with >250 employees. MiCA (RTS on Liquidity Reserves for Asset-Referenced Tokens): Obliges issuers of asset-referenced tokens (ARTs) to maintain a specific liquidity buffer to ensure redemption rights for holders. Previously: No harmonized EU-wide reserve requirements existed for asset-referenced tokens. Now: Issuers must maintain a minimum liquidity buffer of 30% of the reserve assets in highly liquid instruments. Application begins immediately upon publication in the Official Journal; affects stablecoin issuers and crypto-asset service providers (CASPs). Impacts Portuguese fintech investors and digital asset custodians. Official Journal of the EU

Why it matters: The simultaneous enforcement of high-risk AI requirements and expanded sustainability reporting marks a transition from voluntary frameworks to strict, quantifiable compliance for the EU's digital and green economies.
