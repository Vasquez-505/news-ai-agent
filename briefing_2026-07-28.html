Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 amending the EU AI Act and related regulations): Previously, obligations for high-risk AI systems under Annex III were largely set to apply from August 2, 2026, and specific machine-readable marking requirements for AI-generated content were less defined. Now, the application date for stand-alone high-risk AI systems (e.g., in employment, education, critical infrastructure) is deferred to December 2, 2027, while for AI systems embedded in products subject to EU sectoral safety legislation (Annex I), the deadline shifts to August 2, 2028. Providers of AI systems that generate synthetic audio, image, video, or text content must mark outputs in a machine-readable format to be detectable as AI-generated, applying from August 2, 2026, for new systems, with a compliance deadline of December 2, 2026, for systems placed on the market before August 2, 2026. Temporary Regulation on Voluntary Detection of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Previously, the interim regulation allowing voluntary detection and reporting of CSAM by online service providers expired on April 3, 2026, creating a legal ambiguity. Now, the EU Council has reinstated a derogation from data-protection rules in the electronic-communications sector, allowing online service providers to voluntarily detect and report child sexual abuse on their services and remove CSAM. This derogation is effective from July 24, 2026, until April 3, 2028, and applies to online service providers offering number-independent interpersonal communication services (e.g., messaging apps, social media platforms) that choose to exercise this voluntary detection.
