🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

Repeal of the Extraordinary Contribution on Local Accommodation (CEAL): The government signed the measure to abolish the CEAL tax for short-term rentals. Previously: Owners were required to pay an extraordinary tax based on a formula involving the property's area and turnover. Now: This tax is completely eliminated. This directly increases the net annual profitability for short-term rental investors. [Portugal.gov.pt]

Administrative Simplex for Business Licensing: A new decree-law replaces the requirement for "prior authorization" with "prior communication" for a wide range of low-risk commercial activities. Previously: Entrepreneurs had to obtain a municipal license and wait for approval before opening their business. Now: Businesses can open immediately upon submitting a notification to the local authority. This reduces the administrative startup lead time from an average of 180 days to 0 days. [Diário da República]

Reform of the Foreigners' Residence Permit Process: The government has abolished the "manifestation of interest" (manifestação de interesse) mechanism for work permits. Previously: Foreigners could enter Portugal as tourists and subsequently apply for residency after securing employment. Now: Work visas must be applied for and granted at a Portuguese consulate in the home country prior to entry. This eliminates the legal ambiguity and multi-year waiting periods for foreign professionals entering the Portuguese market. [Portugal.gov.pt]

Why it matters: The shift toward deregulation in housing and business licensing, coupled with stricter immigration controls, significantly lowers operational costs for investors while formalizing the labor market.
