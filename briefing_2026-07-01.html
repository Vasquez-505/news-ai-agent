United States (Birthright Citizenship): The U.S. Supreme Court, in a 6-3 ruling on June 30, rejected President Donald Trump's executive order attempting to restrict birthright citizenship, upholding the 14th Amendment's clause for those born in the United States and "subject to the jurisdiction thereof." Authored by Chief Justice John Roberts, the decision was joined by conservative Justices Brett Kavanaugh and Amy Coney Barrett, alongside the court's three liberal justices, blocking Trump's directive that sought to deny citizenship to children born in the U.S. if neither parent was a citizen or legal permanent resident. This ruling marks a significant defeat for President Trump's immigration policies and reaffirms a long-standing interpretation of American citizenship, impacting immigrant families across the country. The U.S. stance on birthright citizenship carries implications for international human rights debates and the legal frameworks of other nations considering similar immigration policies.

🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

Novo Regime de IVA de Grupo: Este regime, que entrou em vigor hoje, 1 de julho de 2026, permite que empresas relacionadas sejam tratadas como uma única entidade para efeitos de Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (IVA). Anteriormente, cada empresa calculava a sua posição de IVA individualmente e as transações internas eram tributáveis; agora, os saldos de IVA podem ser compensados entre os membros do grupo, e as transações internas deixam de ser consideradas para efeitos do imposto. As empresas que pretendam aderir devem ter sede ou estabelecimento permanente em Portugal, realizar atividades que deem origem, total ou parcialmente, à dedução do IVA suportado e operar sob o regime normal de IVA. O impacto prático é a simplificação das obrigações de conformidade fiscal e a melhoria da gestão do fluxo de caixa para grupos empresariais elegíveis.

Reforma do Código dos Contratos Públicos: O Conselho de Ministros aprovou, a 25 de junho de 2026, um Decreto-Lei que concretiza uma reforma abrangente do Código dos Contratos Públicos (CCP). Esta reforma visa modernizar e simplificar o regime da contratação pública, introduzindo medidas para reduzir a burocracia e reforçar a transparência. As mudanças incluem a eliminação da revisão prévia do projeto de execução e o fim da obrigatoriedade do preço base, que eram requisitos anteriores. O impacto prático é que as empresas que participam em concursos públicos devem observar procedimentos simplificados e uma maior eficiência no investimento público.

Gestão Ambiental de Recursos Hídricos (Decreto-Lei n.º 123/2026): Publicado a 24 de junho de 2026, este Decreto-Lei estabelece o regime aplicável à AdP AQUA - Gestão Ambiental de Recursos Hídricos, S. A., com vista à execução da Estratégia «Água Que Une». A AdP AQUA é agora dotada de atribuições e competências para planear, financiar, construir e gerir infraestruturas hidráulicas, hidroagrícolas e de fins múltiplos, bem como infraestruturas de resiliência climática. Anteriormente, a gestão era mais focada em situações de contingência; agora, a AdP AQUA pode atuar de forma mais ágil e célere, incluindo através de processos de expropriação, para promover uma gestão mais eficiente e resiliente da água. O impacto prático é a facilitação de investimentos e ações para melhorar a gestão da água, crucial para o consumo humano, agricultura e indústria.

Designação da Zona Especial de Conservação Fernão Ferro/Lagoa de Albufeira (Decreto-Lei n.º 131/2026): Promulgado a 24 de junho de 2026 e publicado a 30 de junho de 2026, este Decreto-Lei entrou em vigor hoje, 1 de julho de 2026. Conclui o processo de designação da Zona Especial de Conservação (ZEC) Fernão Ferro/Lagoa de Albufeira, especificando o regime jurídico de conservação de habitats e espécies de interesse europeu no âmbito da Rede Natura 2000. Anteriormente, as obrigações de designação das ZEC foram iniciadas com a identificação de sítios da lista nacional; este diploma direciona as medidas de conservação à proteção dos habitats naturais e das espécies que efetivamente existem nesta ZEC específica. O impacto prático é a garantia da proteção ambiental desta área, com simplificação administrativa e salvaguarda dos valores naturais.

Incentivos à Cultura e Reforço de Apoio a Pessoas com Deficiência (Decretos da Assembleia da República): O Presidente da República promulgou, a 30 de junho de 2026, dois decretos da Assembleia da República. Um dos decretos aprova incentivos à promoção, fruição e investimento cultural, alterando a Lei n.º 102/2015, que define o regime jurídico do financiamento colaborativo. O outro reforça a resposta social do serviço de assistência pessoal de apoio à pessoa com deficiência ou incapacidade. O impacto prático é que empresas e cidadãos no setor cultural poderão beneficiar de novos estímulos, e as pessoas com deficiência terão um apoio social melhorado, visando maior autonomia e inclusão.

🇪🇺 EU Policy

Regulation (EU) 2026/1384 on the Union steel market: This Regulation replaces existing EU steel safeguard measures with a comprehensive tariff-rate quota (TRQ) regime. Previously, existing EU steel safeguard measures with a 25% out-of-quota duty applied. Now, an annual total tariff-free quota of 18.3 million tonnes is set for 26 steel product categories, with an increased out-of-quota customs duty of 50% for imports exceeding these quotas. Importers must also provide evidence of the "melt and pour" country of origin by October 1, 2026. This will increase costs for EU businesses importing steel from countries that exceed allocated quotas and requires new supply chain transparency. This directly impacts the cost of raw materials for manufacturing and construction sectors across the EU, including Portuguese industries.

End of EU's €150 Customs Duty De Minimis Threshold: The EU has eliminated the €150 de minimis threshold for customs duty on business-to-consumer (B2C) parcels. Previously, B2C parcels valued under €150 were exempt from customs duties. Now, from July 1, 2026, all B2C goods entering the EU, regardless of value, are subject to customs duty. For IOSS-registered postal consignments under €150, a temporary flat duty of €3 per item will be applied, and mandatory product identifiers (SKU, manufacturer product ID, GTIN/EAN/UPC) will be required from November 1, 2026. This will increase the final cost for consumers on low-value online purchases from outside the EU and significantly raise the administrative and compliance burden for e-commerce businesses, marketplaces, and logistics providers operating in or selling to the EU, including those in Portugal.

Commission Regulation on recycled-content rules for single-use PET beverage bottles: The European Commission has adopted new rules for the calculation, verification, and reporting of recycled content in single-use PET beverage bottles. Previously, there were no specific EU-wide harmonized rules for verifying and reporting recycled content in these bottles. Now, beverage companies operating in the EU must adhere to these new standards. This directly affects beverage producers and importers of single-use PET bottles, requiring adjustments to their supply chains, production processes, and reporting mechanisms to ensure compliance with sustainability targets.

Regulation (EU) 2026/1455 on adjustment of customs duties and tariff quotas for US goods: This Regulation adjusts customs duties on imports of certain goods originating in the United States of America and opens new tariff quotas for these specific goods. This means changes from previous duty rates and quota arrangements. This directly affects businesses that import particular goods from the US, potentially altering their import costs and market access conditions within the EU.

Why it matters: The combination of new steel import controls and the abolition of the €150 customs duty threshold signifies a major shift towards protecting EU industries and enhancing revenue collection, with substantial and immediate impacts on trade, manufacturing, and e-commerce businesses across the Union.

🇺🇸 US Policy

Presidential Proclamation on Phosphate Fertilizer Countervailing Duties: President Donald J. Trump signed a proclamation on June 29, 2026, temporarily suspending countervailing duties (CVDs) of up to 16.8% on certain phosphate fertilizer imports from Morocco for eight months or until the declared emergency is terminated, whichever occurs first. This action, taken under the Tariff Act of 1930, cites an emergency due to insufficient domestic production and global supply chain disruptions. Previously, these imports were subject to CVDs imposed in 2021. Now, they are temporarily duty-free, aiming to stabilize the fertilizer supply chain.

Presidential Memorandum on Expanding the Freedom to Fix Vehicles: On June 29, 2026, President Donald J. Trump signed a Presidential Memorandum directing the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Administrator to issue guidance clarifying actions individuals may take to repair their vehicles' emissions systems in compliance with the Clean Air Act. The memorandum also mandates the EPA to encourage and expedite alternative certification pathways for aftermarket parts and to consider deprioritizing civil enforcement actions against individuals who, in good faith, attempt to restore vehicles to their original configuration. Previously, the clarity around self-repair on emissions systems and aftermarket parts certification was less defined, and civil enforcement might have been more broadly applied. Now, consumers are expected to have clearer guidelines and more options for vehicle repairs.

CFTC Penalizes Foreign Firms for Unregistered Services to US Clients: The Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) ordered two foreign firms, Netrios LP Ltd. (Saint Lucia) and Red Acre Ltd. (Malta), to pay a combined civil monetary penalty of $2.5 million ($1.75 million from Netrios and $750,000 from Red Acre) for operating as unregistered Futures Commission Merchants and offering illegal leveraged retail commodity transactions to U.S. customers. The order, entered on June 26, 2026, and publicly reported on June 29-30, 2026, also mandates them to cease their illegal activities. Previously, these firms operated offshore platforms providing leveraged trading without CFTC registration, in violation of the Commodity Exchange Act. Now, they face significant financial penalties and are prohibited from such unregistered activities.

Joint Sanctions on Hezbollah Financial Network: The U.S. Department of the Treasury, in coordination with the Terrorist Financing Targeting Center (TFTC) member states (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates), announced joint sanctions on June 30, 2026, targeting five entities and 16 individuals linked to Hezbollah's financial infrastructure. Key institutions designated include Al-Qard Al-Hassan and Bayt al-Mal, along with their senior leaders. All designated targets had been previously sanctioned by the United States. Previously, these entities and individuals facilitated financial transactions for Hezbollah. Now, these coordinated international sanctions further disrupt their ability to exploit the international financial system.

Joint Agency Final Rule on Financial Regulatory Data Standards: A joint final rule establishing technical standards for financial regulatory data reporting was issued by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Federal Reserve, Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), National Credit Union Administration (NCUA), Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA), Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and Department of the Treasury. This rule, mandated by the Financial Data Transparency Act of 2022 and reported on June 30, 2026, adopts common identifiers for entities, geographic locations, dates, and certain products/currencies, and establishes a principles-based standard for data transmission and schema/taxonomy formats. Previously, financial regulatory data reporting standards varied more significantly across agencies. Now, the new framework promotes interoperability and aims for high-quality, machine-readable data submissions.
