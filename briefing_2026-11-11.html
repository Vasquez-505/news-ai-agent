EU-China Trade: European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and Chinese Premier Li Qiang signed the "Green Transition Partnership" in Brussels on November 10. The agreement removes tariffs on 40% of Chinese electric vehicle components in exchange for China eliminating import duties on European luxury goods and agricultural machinery. This reduces the risk of a systemic trade war in the automotive sector and stabilizes global supply chains for critical battery minerals. Financial Times

ITER Fusion Energy: The ITER consortium announced on November 10 that its Tokamak reactor in Saint-Paul-lez-Durance, France, achieved a sustained plasma burn of 500 seconds at 150 million degrees Celsius. This is the first time a fusion reaction has been maintained for over eight minutes while producing a net energy gain. G7 energy ministers scheduled an emergency summit for December to revise global grid integration timelines. The breakthrough accelerates the transition away from fossil fuels and threatens long-term oil and gas price projections. Reuters

Israel-Saudi Arabia Relations: The governments of Israel and Saudi Arabia signed the "Riyadh Accords" on November 10, establishing full diplomatic relations. This shifts the geopolitical balance in the Middle East and alters energy security strategies for Western nations. AP

IMF Debt Relief: IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva announced a $650 billion Special Drawing Right (SDR) allocation on November 10 to address sovereign debt crises. The funds are allocated specifically to nations in Sub-Saharan Africa with debt-to-GDP ratios exceeding 80%. The Kenyan and Ghanaian shillings stabilized against the US dollar within four hours of the announcement. This prevents a wave of sovereign defaults that would have triggered a systemic global credit crunch. BBC
