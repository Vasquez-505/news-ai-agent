Regime Extraordinário de Regularização de Casas de Função: Este Decreto-Lei estabelece um regime excecional e temporário, em vigor até 31 de dezembro de 2030, para a regularização de ocupações irregulares de casas de função pertencentes ao domínio privado do Estado e de institutos públicos. Anteriormente, estas casas deveriam ser restituídas aquando da cessação de funções, falecimento ou transferência do funcionário, sob pena de despejo administrativo. Agora, permite a constituição de um direito de habitação para ex-funcionários após aposentação, ou para cônjuges e descendentes (com 65 ou mais anos, ou 60%+ de incapacidade) que residiam no imóvel e não possuem alternativa habitacional digna. O impacto prático é que oferece estabilidade habitacional a indivíduos em situações sociais e humanas vulneráveis que, de outra forma, estariam sujeitos a despejo, regularizando a sua situação.

Esta revisão permitirá clarificar o conceito de donativos (incluindo em espécie), rever os limites de consideração como gastos ou perdas e as percentagens de majoração aplicáveis no mecenato cultural (em sede de IRC e IRS Categoria B), definir as entidades e iniciativas culturais elegíveis e criar um sistema de reconhecimento para estas. O impacto prático é que, embora não altere imediatamente os benefícios fiscais, confere ao Governo a capacidade legal para, num futuro próximo, criar um quadro mais claro e potencialmente mais vantajoso para o investimento privado em cultura e outras áreas de mecenato.

🇪🇺 EU Policy

Regulation on the screening of foreign investments in the Union and repealing Regulation (EU) 2019/452: Previously, Regulation (EU) 2019/452 provided a framework for foreign investment screening without mandating a common EU-wide approach. Now, this new Regulation formally adopted by both the Council and the European Parliament on June 17, 2026, mandates all EU member states to establish and maintain foreign investment screening regimes with a common minimum scope, explicitly covering sensitive sectors, technologies, and infrastructure. It significantly enhances cooperation between national screening authorities and the European Commission. Member states must introduce specific "call-in rights" and ensure minimum post-completion review periods: at least 15 months (extendable up to five years) for authority-initiated screenings, and at least 24 months for investments subject to prior authorization but not filed or filed after completion. The Regulation will be published in the Official Journal and enter into force 20 days after publication, with the new rules beginning to apply 18 months thereafter. This reform aims to strengthen the EU's security and public order, providing a more harmonized, structured, and digitally enabled process for investors, which is particularly relevant for Pedro as an investor engaged in cross-border activities within the EU.

EU-US Trade Tariff Legislation (Parliamentary Approval): On June 16, 2026, the European Parliament formally approved two legislative proposals aimed at implementing aspects of the August 2025 EU-US trade deal. The main proposal eliminates tariffs on all US industrial goods and provides preferential market access for a broad range of US seafood and agricultural goods. The second proposal prolongs tariff-free imports of lobster, now extending to include processed lobster. Previously, various tariffs applied to these categories, with increased tariffs on steel and aluminium derivatives becoming a point of contention. This legislation, once formally approved by the Council and subsequently published in the EU's Official Journal, will enter into force the day after its publication. It includes a sunset clause, with tariff preferences set to expire on December 31, 2029, unless renewed, and allows the Commission to suspend preferences if the US applies tariffs higher than 15% on EU steel and aluminium derivatives by December 31, 2026. This parliamentary approval represents a significant step towards a comprehensive transatlantic trade agreement, potentially reducing costs and expanding market access for EU businesses, including those in Portugal, involved in trade with the US.
