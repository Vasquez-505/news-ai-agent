This implementing regulation, adopted on 29 June 2026 and applying from 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2026, details the country-specific distribution of these quotas, reserving half for preferential trading partners. This will directly impact Portuguese companies involved in steel imports by setting new conditions and limits for tariff-free access.

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1425 on Recycled Plastic Content in Beverage Bottles: This Decision clarifies how recycled plastic content is calculated for the Single Use Plastics Directive (SUPD). Previously, the SUPD mandated a 25% recycled plastic target for beverage bottles, but the language was ambiguous regarding imports; now, as of 23 July 2026, imported recycled polyethylene terephthalate (rPET) will generally not count towards this 25% target until 21 November 2027. Recycled plastic must be from post-consumer plastic waste recycled within the Union to be counted, with provisions for certain third countries from 21 November 2027 subject to specific environmental standards. Portuguese beverage producers and plastics recyclers will need to adjust their sourcing strategies to meet the SUPD's recycled content obligations.

Council Regulation on Artificial Intelligence (Omnibus VII package): The Council has given its final approval to new regulations streamlining and simplifying certain rules concerning Artificial Intelligence. Previously, provisions on high-risk AI systems were due to enter into force on 2 August 2026, and the grace period for transparency solutions for artificially generated content was six months; now, the application date for stand-alone high-risk AI systems is delayed to 2 December 2027, and for high-risk AI systems embedded in products to 2 August 2028. Previously, products containing, consisting of, or produced from genetically modified maize DP202216 x NK603 x DAS-40278-9 and its sub-combinations DP202216 x NK603, DP202216 x DAS-40278-9, required specific authorisation for market placement; now, as of 29 June 2026, this Commission Implementing Decision grants that authorisation, making these products legally available for import and sale within the EU.
