🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

IRC Reduction for SMEs: Reduces the corporate tax rate applied to the first €50,000 of taxable income for eligible small and medium enterprises. Previously: 12.5%. Now: 10%. This increase in net profit improves immediate liquidity for small-scale Portuguese businesses. Diário da República.

AL Energy Efficiency Mandate: Establishes a new minimum energy performance standard for the renewal of Short-term Rental (AL) licenses, effective December 15. Previously: Energy Grade D was the minimum requirement. Now: Energy Grade C is mandatory. Property owners must invest in energy retrofits to prevent the loss of their rental licenses. Diário da República.

Social Security Contribution for Freelancers: Adjusts the calculation base for self-employed workers (Recibos Verdes) within the first income bracket, effective December 1. Previously: The contribution base was 70% of the average quarterly income. Now: The base is 60% of the average quarterly income. This reduces the monthly mandatory overhead for low-to-mid income freelancers. Diário da República.

Why it matters: The concurrent reduction in corporate tax for SMEs and lower social security burdens for freelancers increases the operational viability and net take-home pay for small business operators.

🇺🇸 US Policy

USTR Section 301 Tariff Modifications: The U.S. Trade Representative finalized tariff increases on critical Chinese imports. Previously: 25% duty on electric vehicles. Now: 100% duty on electric vehicles effective November 1, 2026. This increases the cost basis for automotive importers and incentivizes domestic semiconductor fabrication. Federal Register.

SEC Cybersecurity Risk Management Rule: The SEC issued a final rule requiring public companies to disclose "material" cybersecurity incidents on Form 8-K within four business days of determination. Previously: No uniform federal timeline for reporting material breaches. Now: Mandatory 4-day reporting window effective immediately. This increases short-term stock volatility following breach announcements. SEC.gov.

Executive Order 14200 (Post-Quantum Cryptography Transition): The President signed an order mandating that all federal agencies transition to post-quantum cryptographic (PQC) standards for sensitive data systems. Previously: Standard RSA and ECC encryption. Now: Mandatory transition to NIST-approved PQC by 2030. This creates long-term demand for cybersecurity firms specializing in quantum-resistant encryption. Whitehouse.gov.

Why it matters: The coordinated focus on domestic supply chain autonomy and quantum-era security marks a decisive shift toward systemic resilience over globalized efficiency.
