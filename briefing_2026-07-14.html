European Union/Israeli Settlements: European Union foreign ministers convened in Brussels on Monday to discuss potential new measures to curb trade with Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, several months after the EU imposed sanctions on Israel over settlement construction. 🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

Fundo de Emergência para a Habitação (FEH): Este novo Decreto-Lei cria um fundo para apoiar pessoas e famílias em situação de emergência habitacional, como despejos por carência económica ou violência doméstica. O fundo oferece apoio financeiro direto e não reembolsável para garantir o realojamento rápido, temporário ou permanente, garantindo que ninguém fica sem solução imediata em caso de perda de casa. Anteriormente, não existia um fundo estatal específico com esta finalidade. O Estado passa a assumir os custos sociais destas situações, protegendo agregados familiares com rendimentos abaixo de três vezes o salário mínimo nacional (até €2.760/mês em 2026), com apoio que pode chegar a €537,13/mês por seis meses. O impacto prático é uma rede de segurança crucial para os mais vulneráveis em risco de perda de habitação.

Regime de Grupo de IVA (Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado): Entrou em vigor em 1 de julho de 2026, permitindo que empresas relacionadas sejam tratadas como uma única entidade para efeitos de IVA. Anteriormente, as empresas eram tratadas individualmente para o cálculo do IVA. Agora, os saldos de IVA podem ser compensados entre os membros do grupo e as transações internas deixam de ser consideradas para efeitos do imposto, desde que cumpram requisitos de ligação financeira, económica e organizacional. O impacto prático para as empresas é a simplificação da gestão do IVA, a otimização dos fluxos de caixa e a redução da burocracia em transações intragrupo.

Validade da Carta de Condução para Cidadãos Não-UE (Decreto-Lei n.º 114/2026): Este novo Decreto-Lei, reportado em 13 de julho de 2026, vincula a validade da carta de condução portuguesa de cidadãos não-UE (nacionais de países terceiros) à data de validade do seu visto ou título de residência. Anteriormente, a validade da carta de condução poderia ser independente da data de validade do título de residência no documento físico. As novas cartas incluirão o Código de Restrição 794, que indica "Validade limitada à prova de visto ou título de residência válido, ou em processo de renovação". O impacto prático é que os expatriados não-UE devem garantir a validade contínua dos seus títulos de residência para manter a sua carta de condução legalmente ativa em Portugal.

Flexibilização do Arrendamento de Imóveis Públicos (Novo Decreto-Lei): Um novo decreto-lei, introduzido em 12 de julho de 2026, elimina a necessidade de aprovação administrativa de alto nível (Primeiro-Ministro ou ministros relevantes) para contratos de arrendamento de edifícios estatais com renda anual inferior a €7.500 e duração total não superior a seis anos. Anteriormente, qualquer alienação, troca ou arrendamento de infraestruturas estatais exigia um despacho governamental de alto nível. O impacto prático é a simplificação e aceleração dos processos burocráticos para arranjos de propriedade pública de pequena escala e temporários, beneficiando potencialmente empresas ou indivíduos que procuram arrendar estes imóveis.

Prorrogação de Medidas em Resíduos e Amianto (Decreto-Lei n.º 140/2026): Publicado em 10 de julho de 2026 e em vigor a partir de 11 de julho de 2026, este Decreto-Lei prorroga até 30 de setembro de 2026 a suspensão de certas disposições do Regime Geral de Gestão de Resíduos e da exigência de autorização prévia da Autoridade para as Condições do Trabalho (ACT) para trabalhos de demolição ou remoção de amianto. Anteriormente, estas suspensões estariam em risco de expirar. O impacto prático é a continuidade de um regime mais flexível para empresas que operam nos setores de gestão de resíduos e remoção de amianto.

Why it matters: O Fundo de Emergência para a Habitação representa uma nova e direta intervenção estatal para proteger os agregados familiares mais vulneráveis em situações de despejo ou perda de casa.

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1698: This regulation provides emergency financial support for agricultural sectors in Croatia, Cyprus, Portugal, Romania, and Slovenia. Previously, no specific emergency financial support was available for these particular adverse climatic events and natural disasters; now, affected agricultural sectors in these Member States will receive direct financial aid to mitigate losses. This directly benefits Portuguese farmers and agricultural businesses impacted by adverse weather.

Capital Requirements Directive VI (CRD VI) Milestone: As of July 11, 2026, a significant transitional milestone in the implementation of CRD VI has been reached. Previously, non-EU banks and firms could offer certain core banking services to EU customers under existing contracts without necessarily establishing a local branch. Now, for all new contracts related to these core banking services entered into after July 11, 2026, firms must, at a minimum, establish a local branch by January 11, 2027. This change fundamentally reshapes how non-EU financial institutions, including those with Portuguese clients, provide core banking services into the European Union, requiring strategic adjustments to their operational models.

Council Implementing Regulations on Restrictive Measures (e.g., (EU) 2026/1708, (EU) 2026/1710, (EU) 2026/1714, (EU) 2026/1720): Several Council Implementing Regulations were published on July 13, 2026, updating and bringing into force restrictive measures concerning the situation in Russia, cyber-attacks threatening the Union or its Member States, and serious human rights violations and abuses. Previously, existing restrictive measures were in place; now, these updated regulations implement specific, directly binding sanctions and related obligations. Businesses and investors must immediately comply with these updated sanctions, which can impact trade, financial transactions, and investment dealings with entities or individuals subject to these measures.

Reinstatement of Interim Rules for Online Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) Detection (ePrivacy derogation): On July 9, 2026, EU lawmakers backed the reinstatement of temporary rules allowing online platforms to voluntarily detect and remove child sexual abuse materials. Previously, these temporary rules (Regulation (EU) 2021/1232) had expired on April 3, 2026, leaving a gap in legal certainty for platforms; now, the ePrivacy derogation is extended until April 3, 2028, permitting voluntary scanning with an explicit exemption for end-to-end encrypted communications. This impacts major tech companies (like Google and Meta) operating in the EU by providing legal cover for voluntary CSAM detection, while also addressing privacy concerns by protecting encrypted messaging.

EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) Product Scope Measures: On July 13, 2026, the European Commission adopted measures clarifying and updating the product scope of the EU Deforestation Regulation. Previously, the EUDR applied to an initial list of commodities; now, the adopted measures remove certain products (e.g., cattle hides, skins, leather, re-treaded tyres, soybeans for sowing) and add others (e.g., soluble coffee, certain palm oil derivatives, frozen cattle tongues) to the scope. While new products will become subject to the regulation from December 30, 2027, this adoption provides immediate legal certainty and predictability for businesses in affected supply chains, allowing them to prepare for future compliance.

🇺🇸 US Policy

National Monument Modifications (Bears Ears & Grand Staircase-Escalante): On July 13, 2026, President Trump signed two proclamations significantly reducing the size of Bears Ears National Monument by approximately 91% (from 1.36 million acres to 121,100 acres) and Grand Staircase-Escalante National Monument by approximately 90% (from 1.87 million acres to 181,500 acres). Previously, these monuments encompassed larger areas with extensive conservation protections; now, their reduced boundaries are intended to allow for "multiple-use, sustained-yield management," potentially opening areas for energy development and mining. This action indicates a policy shift toward greater resource utilization on public lands.

EPA Regulatory Relief for Critical Chemical Manufacturing: On July 13, 2026, President Trump signed a proclamation granting two years of regulatory relief from stringent Biden-era Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) regulations for certain chemical manufacturers critical to national security, including those producing chemicals for semiconductor production, medical device sterilization, advanced manufacturing, and national defense systems. Previously, these facilities faced demanding Biden-era EPA compliance requirements; now, for a period of two years, they are permitted to adhere to the EPA standards that were in place before the Biden Administration rulemakings. This aims to reduce compliance costs and operational disruptions for essential national security industries.

21st Century ROAD to Housing Act Enacted: On July 10, 2026, the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act became law without the President's signature after the constitutional 10-day period for presidential action expired. This bipartisan legislative package, comprising nearly 60 standalone bills, modernizes Federal Housing Administration (FHA) loan limits, expands financing options for manufactured homes and accessory dwelling units (ADUs), streamlines environmental reviews for Housing and Urban Development (HUD)-funded housing projects, and permanently authorizes the Community Development Block Grant Disaster Recovery program. This legislation is expected to increase housing supply, lower housing costs, and modernize federal housing programs to improve housing attainability.

Interagency Guidance on Lending to Non–Work Authorized Borrowers: On July 13, 2026, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), and National Credit Union Administration (NCUA) jointly issued interagency guidance, in accordance with Executive Order 14406, reminding supervised financial institutions to apply existing safe-and-sound credit risk management practices when lending to individuals not legally authorized to work in the United States. Previously, financial institutions applied general credit risk principles; now, the guidance explicitly advises that such borrowers may present elevated credit risk due to potential uncertainties regarding income stability, employment continuity, and overall financial stability, prompting a clarified approach to underwriting.

New Sanctions on Cuban Entities: On July 13, 2026, the U.S. Department of State designated ten Cuban entities under Executive Order 14404, including the tourism ministry, a major hotel operator, and various companies within the trade, energy, ocean transport, and finance sectors. Previously, these specific entities were not directly targeted by U.S. sanctions; now, foreign persons engaging in transactions with these designated entities or operating in the identified sectors of the Cuban economy face increased sanctions risk. This action further tightens economic pressure on the Cuban government, aiming to disrupt its revenue streams and tools of oppression.
