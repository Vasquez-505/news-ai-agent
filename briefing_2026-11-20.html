🇪🇺 EU Policy

EU AI Act: Requires providers of "high-risk" AI systems to implement mandatory risk management, data governance, and human oversight; prohibits "unacceptable risk" AI such as social scoring and certain biometric categorization. Previously: No harmonized EU-wide AI regulation. Now: A tiered risk framework where prohibited systems are banned within 6 months of entry, General Purpose AI (GPAI) transparency rules apply in 12 months, and high-risk obligations apply in 24 months, with fines up to €35M or 7% of global turnover. Affects all AI providers and deployers operating in the EU. Particularly relevant for Portuguese tech hubs and FinTech firms integrating generative AI. [Council of the EU]

Nature Restoration Law: Requires Member States to put in place restoration measures for at least 20% of EU land and sea areas by 2030 and all ecosystems in need of restoration by 2050. Previously: Voluntary nature recovery targets and non-binding guidelines. Now: Legally binding requirement to submit National Restoration Plans with specific targets for peatlands, forests, and urban greenery. Affects EU Member States and large-scale landowners. Significant impact on Portuguese agricultural land-use and coastal zone management. [Council of the EU]

Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD): Mandates companies to identify, prevent, and mitigate adverse human rights and environmental impacts across their own operations and global value chains. Previously: Voluntary Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) reporting and fragmented national laws. Now: Mandatory due diligence for companies with >1,000 employees and >€450M annual turnover, with fines up to 5% of net global turnover. Compliance begins in stages between 2027 and 2029 depending on company size. Affects large EU and non-EU firms operating in the EU. Critical for large Portuguese exporters and energy companies (e.g., Galp, EDP). [Council of the EU]

Why it matters: The formal approval of the AI Act establishes the world's first comprehensive legal framework for artificial intelligence, creating immediate audit obligations for tech providers and high financial risks for non-compliance.
