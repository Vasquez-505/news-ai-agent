SEC AI Trading Disclosure Rule: The SEC issued a final rule requiring investment advisers with assets under management (AUM) exceeding $1 billion to disclose specific AI models used in algorithmic trading and their associated risk mitigation protocols. Previously: AI usage disclosure was voluntary and descriptive. Now: Mandatory quarterly reporting of model versions and risk audits is required. Effective January 1, 2027. Section 301 Solid-State Battery Tariffs: The USTR enacted a tariff increase on high-capacity solid-state batteries imported from designated non-market economies. Previously: Tariffs were set at 5%. Now: Tariffs are increased to 25%. Effective immediately. This creates a price advantage for domestic battery manufacturers while increasing short-term input costs for EV OEMs. [USTR.gov]

EO 14310 on Federal Cybersecurity Resilience: The President signed EO 14310, mandating that all federal contractors with contracts exceeding $10 million implement a verified Zero Trust architecture. Previously: Zero Trust was a recommended framework under NIST guidelines. Now: It is a mandatory requirement for contract eligibility and renewal. Effective October 1, 2026. This will likely drive significant procurement spending toward cybersecurity firms specializing in identity and access management. [whitehouse.gov]

Why it matters: The US government is aggressively transitioning from voluntary guidelines to mandatory enforcement in the realms of AI financial oversight and critical infrastructure security.
