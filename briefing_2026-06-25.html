Despite the White House considering the Senate resolution unconstitutional and not binding, it marks a clear rebuke of President Trump's Iran policy by Congress, while mediated talks have led to a memorandum of understanding and a gradual resumption of shipping, though full liquefied natural gas (LNG) production from Qatar is expected to normalize only within a few weeks. The secure and unhindered passage through the Strait of Hormuz, which facilitates approximately 20% of global petroleum liquids consumption and significant LNG shipments, is critically important for global energy security, international trade, and market stability.

Venezuela: On Wednesday, June 24, 2026, Venezuela was struck by back-to-back powerful earthquakes, with the US Geological Survey (USGS) reporting a magnitude 7.2 tremor near Moron, about 168 km (104 miles) west of Caracas, immediately followed by a magnitude 7.5 quake in the same vicinity. These seismic events caused the collapse of four buildings in Caracas's Chacao district and resulted in at least three confirmed fatalities, while 22 individuals were rescued. This significant natural disaster compounds existing humanitarian challenges in Venezuela, a nation already grappling with severe economic instability and political turmoil, potentially leading to an exacerbated crisis in public health, infrastructure, and essential resource distribution.

Given Japan's critical role in global supply chains and its location in the highly seismic Pacific Ring of Fire, any significant seismic event raises international concerns regarding potential disruptions and the ongoing assurance of nuclear safety protocols, particularly in the shadow of the 2011 Fukushima disaster.

Diferimento de Contribuições à Segurança Social para Transportes: A Portaria n.º 266/2026/1, de 18 de junho, estabelece os procedimentos para uma medida excecional de diferimento do pagamento das contribuições à segurança social devidas nos meses de maio, junho e julho de 2026. Esta medida abrange operadores de transporte de mercadorias por conta de outrem que utilizem combustíveis fósseis e possuam inspeção periódica obrigatória válida, que, de outra forma, teriam de pagar as suas contribuições nas datas habituais. A iniciativa visa aliviar a pressão financeira sobre estes operadores, afetados pelo aumento dos preços dos combustíveis.

Reprogramação de Financiamento do Programa Menos Ruído: A Resolução do Conselho de Ministros n.º 126/2026, de 18 de junho, altera a Resolução n.º 58/2025 para reprogramar o financiamento do Programa Menos Ruído. Os encargos máximos globais de 10.000.000,00 € (isentos de IVA) serão agora divididos pelos anos económicos de 2026 (5.000.000,00 €) e 2027 (5.000.000,00 €), sendo suportados pelo Fundo Ambiental, dado que a verba inicialmente prevista para 2025 não foi executada. Garante a continuidade do programa de isolamento acústico para edifícios habitacionais próximos do Aeroporto Humberto Delgado, com um novo calendário de financiamento.

Prorrogação do Prazo de Adaptação para Casas de Acolhimento: A Portaria n.º 269/2026/1, de 19 de junho, prorroga o prazo para as casas de acolhimento em funcionamento se adaptarem às condições estabelecidas na Portaria n.º 450/2023, de 22 de dezembro, até 30 de setembro de 2028. Anteriormente, o prazo definido pela Portaria n.º 197/2025/1, de 21 de abril, era mais curto. Esta medida concede mais tempo às instituições para realizar os investimentos necessários e aceder a apoios como o Programa de Alargamento da Rede de Equipamentos Sociais - 3.ª Geração (PARES 3.0).

Anteriormente, a Diretiva 2002/59/CE não exigia a comunicação destas informações adicionais. A medida reforça a segurança marítima e a prevenção de poluição por navios, especialmente os que transportam cargas perigosas.

🇪🇺 EU Policy

Amendments to the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act): Previously, the EU AI Act, adopted in 2024, established a comprehensive regulatory framework for Artificial Intelligence, with most obligations for "high-risk" AI systems set to apply from August 2, 2026. The European Parliament has now approved amendments to this Act, aiming to simplify compliance obligations, enhance legal certainty, and reduce the regulatory burden for businesses. Key changes include delays to the application dates for certain obligations pertaining to "high-risk" AI systems and a new ban, effective from December 2, 2026, on AI systems capable of generating child sexual abuse material or non-consensual intimate content. This provides more time and potentially clearer guidelines for tech companies and investors in the AI sector to adapt their compliance strategies.

EU Returns Policy Reform: Previously, the return procedures for non-EU nationals staying illegally in a Member State varied across the EU. The European Parliament has formally approved a new reform of the EU returns policy, based on a provisional political agreement reached with the Council. This reform introduces new obligations for non-EU nationals to cooperate with return authorities, and permits detention for up to 24 months, with potential extensions, for those not cooperating, at risk of absconding, or posing a security risk. It also strengthens the mutual recognition of return decisions across the EU and allows for the establishment of "return hubs" in third countries. This policy will lead to more streamlined and stricter migration management procedures across the EU, impacting social and labour policies, including in Portugal.

Council Decision (EU) 2026/1381 on EU-Canada Procurement under SAFE Instrument: Previously, Canadian legal entities and products did not have explicitly defined conditions for participation in EU public procurement under the Single Aviation Market (SAFE) Instrument. Now, this Council Decision, formally approved and published in the Official Journal of the EU on June 18, 2026, concludes an Agreement between the European Union and Canada. This Agreement lays down the specific conditions for the participation of Canadian legal entities and products originating in Canada in procurement processes under the SAFE Instrument. This fosters increased competition in the EU aviation procurement market and may affect Portuguese businesses bidding for relevant contracts.

Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/1339 correcting Maximum Residue Levels for Fenpyrazamine: Previously, maximum residue levels (MRLs) for the pesticide fenpyrazamine in or on certain products were established under Regulation (EC) No 396/2005. This Commission Regulation, formally approved and published in the Official Journal of the EU on June 18, 2026, corrects these MRLs for fenpyrazamine in or on specific agricultural products. This requires producers, importers, and retailers in the agriculture and food sectors to ensure compliance with the newly corrected residue levels to meet food safety standards within the EU.

Executive Order: Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks: President Donald Trump signed an Executive Order on June 22, 2026, mandating an accelerated, nationwide migration to post-quantum cryptography (PQC) for all federal information systems to protect sensitive data, critical infrastructure, and the digital economy from future quantum computing threats. The order directs the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) and the National Cyber Director to lead this transition, with federal agencies required to designate PQC migration leads and transition high-value assets to PQC by 2030 and 2031 for specific use cases. This action creates a significant, multi-year mandate for federal agencies and a strong incentive for critical infrastructure operators to adopt new cryptographic standards, impacting cybersecurity and technology sectors.

CBP Interim Final Rule: Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption: Effective June 24, 2026, U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) issued an interim final rule that indefinitely suspends the de minimis exemption, which previously allowed merchandise valued at $800 or less to enter the U.S. free of duties and taxes. This suspension applies to goods arriving through all modes of transportation except the international postal network, implementing Executive Order 14324. Previously, most imports under $800 were exempt from duties and taxes; now, they will be subject to applicable duties and taxes, effective immediately. This significant trade policy change will increase costs and administrative burden for importers, particularly e-commerce businesses, and could impact consumer prices for imported goods, while potentially boosting domestic manufacturing.

OFAC Issues General License X Authorizing Certain Iran-Related Transactions: On June 22, 2026, the U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) issued Iran-related General License X. This license authorizes certain transactions, including the export of Iranian crude oil, petroleum products and derivatives, and all associated services (such as banking transactions, insurance, and transportation), effectively relaxing sanctions to implement a U.S.-Iran Memorandum of Understanding. Previously, transactions involving Iranian crude oil and petroleum products were generally prohibited under U.S. sanctions; now, specific transactions related to their export and associated services are authorized. This action represents a significant shift in U.S. sanctions policy towards Iran, potentially leading to increased Iranian oil supply in global markets and opening new avenues for companies involved in energy trading and related services.
