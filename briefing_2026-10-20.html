🌍 World News

[USA/China]: President Julian Vance signed the "Global Chip Sovereignty Act" on October 19, imposing a 40% tariff on all AI-integrated hardware imported from China. This legislation targets $120 billion in annual trade to reduce US dependency on East Asian semiconductors. China's Ministry of Commerce responded on October 20 by restricting the export of gallium and germanium to all US-based firms. This disrupts the global AI hardware supply chain and increases production costs for technology firms worldwide. [Financial Times]

[Ethiopia/Sudan]: Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed and Sudanese General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan signed the "Nile Water Sharing Accord" in Cairo on October 19. The treaty mandates a 15-year water-sharing quota and grants Sudan oversight of 20% of the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam's turbine output. The African Union confirmed the immediate withdrawal of 5,000 standby troops from the border region. This stabilizes the Horn of Africa and reduces the risk of water-driven conflict for 100 million people. [Reuters]

[ITER/France]: The ITER consortium in Saint-Paul-lès-Duelmen achieved a sustained nuclear fusion reaction of 500 megawatts for 300 seconds on October 19. This event marks the first time a plasma temperature of 150 million degrees Celsius was maintained beyond the five-minute threshold. The European Commission announced a €10 billion acceleration fund to transition this technology to commercial grids by 2032. This proves the viability of nuclear fusion as a carbon-free baseload power source for the global energy grid. [BBC]

[ECB/Germany]: President Christine Lagarde announced a 50 basis point interest rate cut to 2.25% during a press conference in Frankfurt on October 20. The decision follows the Eurozone's inflation rate dropping to 1.8%, the first time it has remained below the 2% target for three consecutive months. The Euro fell 0.4% against the US Dollar immediately following the announcement. This signals a strategic shift in European monetary policy from inflation control to economic growth stimulation. [Deutsche Welle]

🇵🇹 Portugal Policy

Redução da Retenção na Fonte de IRS: O Governo decidiu reduzir em 50% as retenções na fonte aplicáveis aos rendimentos do trabalho dependente e às pensões pagas durante o mês de novembro de 2026, incluindo o subsídio de Natal devido nesse mês. Anteriormente, aplicavam-se as tabelas de retenção habituais. Esta medida visa permitir que as famílias beneficiem imediatamente da descida das taxas de IRS nos primeiros seis escalões, aprovada pelo Parlamento com efeitos desde 1 de janeiro de 2026, sem ter de aguardar pelo acerto anual em 2027.

Novos Limites para Rendas Acessíveis: A Portaria n.º 454/2026/1, publicada em 13 de outubro de 2026 e em vigor desde 14 de outubro de 2026, estabelece os limites máximos de renda por concelho e tipologia (T0 a T5+) para o Regime Simplificado de Arrendamento Acessível (RSAA). Por exemplo, o limite de renda para um T2 em Lisboa é de 1.309 euros e no Porto de 1.118 euros. Anteriormente, os proprietários não tinham valores de referência concretos para este regime. Esta portaria permite aos proprietários conhecer os valores para se candidatarem à isenção de IRS ou IRC sobre os rendimentos prediais, desde que cumpram as condições do RSAA.

Alterações ao Código dos Contratos Públicos (CCP): O Decreto-Lei n.º 177/2026, de 4 de setembro, que entrou em vigor a 1 de outubro de 2026, simplifica os procedimentos de contratação pública ao aumentar significativamente os limites para o recurso a ajustes diretos e consultas prévias. Para empreitadas de obras públicas, o limite para consulta prévia subiu de 150 mil para 1 milhão de euros, e para ajuste direto de 30 mil para 150 mil euros. Para aquisição de bens e serviços, a consulta prévia passou de 75 mil para 130 mil euros e o ajuste direto de 20 mil para 75 mil euros.

Novas Regras para o Uso de Bandeiras em Edifícios Públicos: A Lei n.º 63/2026, publicada em 19 de outubro de 2026 e em vigor desde 20 de outubro de 2026, estabelece que em edifícios e instalações públicas, incluindo nas regiões autónomas, é exclusivamente permitida a exibição da Bandeira Nacional, da bandeira da União Europeia, e de bandeiras institucionais e heráldicas das entidades públicas. A lei determina a remoção de bandeiras não autorizadas e prevê coimas até quatro mil euros. Anteriormente, as regras eram menos explícitas e abrangentes, permitindo maior diversidade de símbolos.

Prorrogação de Limites de Taxas de Imposto sobre Produtos Petrolíferos e Energéticos: O Presidente da República promulgou, em 13 de outubro de 2026, o Decreto da Assembleia da República n.º 114/XVII, que prorroga a vigência da Lei n.º 12-B/2026, de 15 de abril. Esta lei altera temporária e excecionalmente os limites mínimos das taxas unitárias do imposto sobre os produtos petrolíferos e energéticos.

Why it matters: As famílias portuguesas terão um alívio temporário no rendimento disponível em novembro devido à redução da retenção na fonte do IRS, enquanto o mercado de arrendamento acessível ganha maior clareza com novos limites de renda.

🇪🇺 EU Policy

EU AI Act (High-Risk Systems Implementation): Providers of high-risk AI systems (Annex III) must now complete mandatory conformity assessments and register their systems in the EU database. Previously: No mandatory pre-market conformity assessment existed for AI. Now: Systems used in critical infrastructure, employment, and education must meet strict risk management and data governance standards to remain on the market as of October 2026. This creates significant compliance costs for Portuguese AI startups specializing in HR and healthcare tech. EUR-Lex

Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) Financial Phase: Importers of cement, iron, steel, aluminum, fertilizers, and electricity must now purchase and surrender CBAM certificates to cover embedded carbon emissions. Previously: Only quarterly reporting of emissions was required during the transitional phase. Now: Financial payments for carbon leakage are mandatory as of October 2026. This will directly increase the cost of raw material imports for Portuguese industrial and construction firms. European Commission

Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) Liquidity Requirements: Issuers of "significant" asset-referenced tokens (ARTs) must maintain a reserve of assets where at least 30% consists of highly liquid assets. Previously: Reserve requirements were fragmented and determined by individual national regulators. Now: A uniform EU-wide threshold of 30% liquidity for reserves is mandatory for tokens exceeding 10 million holders. This reduces systemic risk for Portuguese retail investors holding stablecoins. EUR-Lex

Why it matters: The shift from reporting to financial enforcement in CBAM and the activation of high-risk AI mandates move the EU's "Twin Transition" from a regulatory framework to a direct operational cost for businesses.

🇺🇸 US Policy

Executive Order 14210 (Strategic Mineral Resilience): The President signed an order mandating the Department of Defense and Department of Energy to expand national reserves of lithium and cobalt. Previously: National reserves were maintained at a 90-day supply threshold. Now: Reserves must reach a 120-day supply by December 31, 2027, effective immediately. This is expected to increase domestic procurement volume and support price floors for US-based mineral miners. White House

SEC Final Rule 2026-12 (Digital Asset Custody Disclosure): The SEC enacted a final rule requiring public companies to disclose the specific legal entity and jurisdiction of custodians for digital assets valued over $10 million. Previously: Custody disclosure was discretionary and typically provided in general terms. Now: Mandatory identification of the custodian is required for assets exceeding the $10 million threshold, effective January 1, 2027. This increases transparency for investors regarding the counterparty risk of corporate digital holdings. Federal Register

Treasury OFAC Sanctions (Sector-Z Tech Entities): The Treasury Department designated 12 semiconductor firms as Specially Designated Nationals (SDN), prohibiting all US financial transactions with these entities. Previously: These firms were subject to limited export licenses for specific high-end hardware. Now: All US transactions with these 12 firms are prohibited, effective immediately. This may disrupt the supply of legacy-node semiconductors for US industrial manufacturers. Reuters US Politics

Why it matters: The US is implementing a dual-track strategy of increasing physical commodity reserves while tightening the regulatory perimeter around digital assets and foreign tech dependencies.
